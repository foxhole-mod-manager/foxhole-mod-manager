Foxhole Mod Manager (unofficial)
================================
Fan-made. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Siege Camp. Foxhole is a trademark of Siege Camp.

PLAY - starts the live war through Steam with the cosmetic mods you switched on.
MODS - cosmetic mods for Foxhole (.pak and settings files): add, switch on / off, remove; each is checked against your game version.
BROWSE MODS - Foxhole mods on Nexus Mods; Download opens the mod's page in your own browser.

The offline sandbox of version 1.0.0 is removed for now. If you used it: open the app once and press RESTORE THE GAME (shown while
the app finds the sandbox's changes in your Foxhole) - it puts the original files back. Steam's "Verify integrity of game files" also
repairs the game.

Uninstall: switch your mods off if you want them gone from the game, then delete this folder and %LOCALAPPDATA%\FoxholeModManager
(your mod list and settings).
